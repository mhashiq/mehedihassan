I am a **Ph.D. Researcher & Graduate Research Assistant** at **Texas Tech University** (College of Health & Human Sciences), specializing in **AI-driven medical imaging**, **computer vision**, and **digital health diagnostics** for obesity, metabolic disorders, fatty liver diseases (MASLD/NAFLD), type 2 diabetes mellitus, and chronic health conditions.

Holding M.Sc. and B.Sc. degrees in Computer Science and Engineering, my research bridges deep learning innovation with clinical workflow optimization to build scalable diagnostic tools and advance computational healthcare education.
